MEAM620 Advanced Robotics

This project focused on the design and implementation of autonomous quadcopter systems, where the primary objective was to develop components required for UAV flight, including feedback control, motion planning, trajectory generation, and state estimation. The end goal was to allow quadcopters to navigate complex three-dimensional environments while maintaining stability, avoiding collisions, and optimizing flight performance
